FM-1 Hub turns your iPhone into a firmware manager for the M-VAVE FM-1 synthesizer. Browse the community's open-source firmwares, plug the FM-1 in over USB, and install one in about a minute. No computer, no account.

BROWSE
• The full catalogue of The FM-1 Firmware Hub: synths, grooveboxes, drum machines and samplers, chord instruments, trackers
• Cards with each firmware's own screen, maker, version, popularity and description
• Search, filters by type and style, sorting, list or grid
• Details for every firmware: release date, size, licence, checksum, links to its project, source, editor and manual

INSTALL
• One tap to put a firmware on your FM-1 over USB
• Every package is checked against its published SHA-256 before a single byte is written
• An interrupted install continues where it stopped
• One tap back to M-VAVE's official firmware, straight from M-VAVE's own download server

BACK UP AND RESTORE
• One-tap backup of the state of your FM-1: which firmware it runs and the firmware package itself, found automatically
• On firmwares of the Felucca family, your music too: projects, presets, settings and samples
• One-tap restore
• Backups live on your phone and in the Files app, ready to copy to iCloud Drive

HISTORY
• Every install remembered: what went on, when, and whether it worked

PRIVATE
• No account, no tracking, no analytics. Everything stays on your phone.

REQUIREMENTS
• An M-VAVE FM-1 and a USB cable (iPhone 15 and newer: USB-C; older iPhones: Apple's Lightning to USB camera adapter)
