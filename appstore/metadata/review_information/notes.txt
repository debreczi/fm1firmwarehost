FM-1 Hub installs community firmware on the M-VAVE FM-1, a small USB MIDI synthesizer, and backs it up and restores it. It is free, with no account, ads, in-app purchases or data collection.

SCREEN RECORDING
The attached video (5 minutes, on an iPhone with a real FM-1, build 1.0 (20261102.24)) starts on the Home Screen. It shows browsing the catalogue, connecting the FM-1, backing it up, installing SLOOP 2.8.4 from the catalogue (the app backs up the music first), then restoring the earlier backup: the firmware the FM-1 had before (SLOOP 2.8.1) and its music.

PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
For owners of the M-VAVE FM-1. Independent developers write alternative firmwares for it: synthesizers, grooveboxes, drum machines. The app lets an owner pick one, install it over USB from an iPhone, and keep backups so the previous firmware and their music can be put back.

SETUP
1. An M-VAVE FM-1, switched on.
2. iPhone 15 or later: a USB-C to USB-C cable. Earlier iPhones: Apple's Lightning to USB Camera Adapter.
3. Open the app and accept the risk notice shown on first launch.
4. FM-1 tab > Connect. The app shows which firmware the FM-1 runs.
5. Try "Back up the FM-1 now", then Install on a firmware in the Firmwares tab, then Backups > the backup > "Restore the firmware".

Without the device, the Firmwares tab (catalogue, search, filters, detail pages), the FM-1 tab (Connect reports that no FM-1 was found), Backups and History (empty until used) and Settings can be reviewed. Installing, backing up and restoring need the FM-1.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
There is no server, account or analytics of our own. The app downloads public files only:
- thefirmwarehub.com: the firmware catalogue, firmware packages and pictures
- api.github.com and github.com: a firmware's release files from its maker, when a backup needs a version the catalogue doesn't have
- M-VAVE's download server (yms-file-store.oss-cn-hongkong.aliyuncs.com): M-VAVE's official firmware
Links to project pages, web editors and feedback open in Safari. Downloaded firmware never runs on the iPhone: the app only sends it to the FM-1 over USB MIDI, with the same update protocol M-VAVE's own updater uses.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app is in English and works the same in every region.
