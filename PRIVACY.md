# Privacy policy — FM-1 Hub

_Last updated: 28 October 2026_

**FM-1 Hub collects no personal data.** There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What stays on your iPhone

Your settings, install history, downloaded firmware packages and backups are stored only on your device, in the app's
own storage (visible in the Files app under "On My iPhone › FM-1 Hub"). They are never uploaded by the app. If you
copy them to iCloud Drive or elsewhere yourself, that service's own terms apply.

## Network requests the app makes

To do its job the app downloads public files. These requests carry no identifier from the app; like any web request,
the servers involved see your IP address.

| Server | What is downloaded |
| --- | --- |
| thefirmwarehub.com | the firmware catalogue, firmware packages and pictures |
| api.github.com, github.com | firmware release files published by their makers |
| m-vave.com (yms-file-store.oss-cn-hongkong.aliyuncs.com) | M-VAVE's official FM-1 firmware |

Links you open from the app (project pages, editors) open in Safari and are subject to those sites' policies.

## USB

The app talks to your FM-1 over USB MIDI only to identify it, install firmware and read or write backups. Nothing it
reads from the device leaves your phone.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
