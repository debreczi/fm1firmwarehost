# Support — FM-1 Hub

**Something went wrong?** Open an issue at https://github.com/debreczi/fm1firmwarehost/issues with:

- what you tapped, and what the app showed (a screenshot helps),
- the text from the FM-1 tab › "More and log",
- your iPhone model and iOS version.

## Common questions

**The FM-1 is not found.** Use a data-capable cable (iPhone 15 and newer: USB-C to USB-C; older iPhones: Apple's
Lightning to USB camera adapter). Switch the FM-1 on, close other MIDI apps, then press Connect. If your firmware
names its USB port after itself, tap "This is my FM-1" next to its name.

**An install stopped halfway.** Plug the FM-1 back in and press Install again: it continues where it stopped. If the
FM-1 does not start any more, hold OCT− while switching it on and install again.

**Back to stock.** FM-1 tab › Official firmware.

**Where are my backups?** Backups tab, and in the Files app under "On My iPhone › FM-1 Hub › Snapshots".

The knowledge base of The FM-1 Firmware Hub covers firmware-specific questions: https://thefirmwarehub.com/kb/
